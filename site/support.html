Kod Remote support

Kod Remote is the iPhone companion to Kod, the Mac app that runs your claude and codex sessions. It shows which ones need you, and lets you answer them from your phone.

Pairing

  1. On your Mac, open Kod → Settings → Mobile and turn on Serve my sessions to my phone.
  2. Choose how your phone reaches the Mac: My Wi-Fi network, My Tailscale network, or both.
  3. On your iPhone, open Kod Remote, tap Pair, and scan the code Kod shows. No camera handy? Use Copy pairing link on the Mac and Paste in the app.

Want to look around first? Explore with sample data shows the whole app with made-up sessions, no Mac needed.

If it will not connect

  • "No answer" on Wi-Fi. Check that the Mac is awake and on the same network, and that Kod Remote is allowed under iOS Settings → Kod → Local Network. A "Don't Allow" there is remembered, and silently blocks every connection.
  • Away from home. Turn on Tailscale on both devices and My Tailscale network on the Mac, then pair again so the code carries the Tailscale address too.
  • "Nothing is listening on that port". Kod is not serving the phone: check the switch in Settings → Mobile, and that Kod is running.
  • "Token rejected" or "a different key". The token was regenerated, or this is not the Mac you paired with. Scan a fresh code.
Your sessions travel only between your phone and your Mac, encrypted and pinned to your Mac's key. Anyone holding the pairing code can type into your sessions, so treat it like a password — and if it may have been seen, use Regenerate token on the Mac. More in our privacy policy.
